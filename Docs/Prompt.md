\# Build “SafarSet” – AI-Powered Smart Travel Planning Platform for India



Design and develop a complete, professional, production-style full-stack web application named \*\*SafarSet\*\*.



\*\*Tagline:\*\* “Plan Karo. Safar Set Karo.”



SafarSet is an India-focused intelligent travel planning platform that helps users plan complete trips based on their destination, dates, budget, travel purpose, transport preference, accommodation preference, meetings, activities, food interests and available free time.



The platform should combine:



\* Trip planning

\* Multi-city itinerary management

\* Transport planning

\* Hotel discovery and comparison

\* Sightseeing and activities

\* Business meeting scheduling

\* Nearby exploration

\* Budget management

\* AI itinerary generation

\* AI-powered rescheduling

\* Food discovery

\* Optional reminders

\* Taxi booking

\* Local guide booking

\* Emergency assistance

\* Travel memories

\* Reviews

\* Trip sharing

\* PDF trip reports



The website must feel like a \*\*real premium travel-tech startup product\*\*, not a generic AI-generated dashboard.



\---



\# 1. DESIGN DIRECTION



Create a modern, clean, visually rich and highly professional travel interface.



The design should feel inspired by modern travel applications such as itinerary planners, booking platforms and premium travel dashboards, but \*\*do not directly copy any existing application\*\*.



Use:



\* Clean typography

\* Generous but controlled spacing

\* Rounded cards

\* Subtle shadows

\* High-quality destination photography

\* Smooth hover effects

\* Micro-interactions

\* Skeleton loading states

\* Empty states

\* Toast notifications

\* Professional icons

\* Responsive navigation

\* Mobile-friendly layouts



Avoid:



\* Excessive gradients

\* Random glowing elements

\* Huge text everywhere

\* Excessive animations

\* Generic AI-generated cards

\* Too many emojis

\* Cluttered dashboards



The interface should feel like something users could genuinely use for planning their trips.
